## Identity marks observed — TWO COMPETING LOCKUPS

**Mark A — profile avatar**
- Deep burgundy circle, gold palm-tree icon above a decorative gold script/serif "Rare Dates"
- Stacked, ornamental, warm, traditional-Gulf feel

**Mark B — feed post (video cover)**

are unearned superlatives with no proof behind them.

## RISK — flagged, requires a client decision
Instagram has labelled both posts **"AI content"** and the caption confirms the product
imagery is AI-generated. For a luxury *food* brand this is a live problem:
1. The public "AI content" tag sits directly under the brand name and contradicts
   "handcrafted / finest / crafted".
2. The rendered dates do not depict the actual product a customer will receive —
   a consumer-protection exposure in Jordan and a returns/complaints driver.
3. It caps the brand: no press, stockist, hotel or corporate buyer will use AI renders.

Recommendation: a real photo shoot before launch. Until then, AI renders must not be
used anywhere a customer makes a purchase decision — least of all the website.
